# Demo Shop Requirements

## REQ-LOGIN
As a registered user I can sign in with email and password and reach the dashboard.

Criticality: HIGH

## REQ-VALIDATION
Product creation rejects missing name, invalid email contact, and non-positive price.

Criticality: MEDIUM

## REQ-CHECKOUT
An authenticated shopper can add a product to the cart and complete checkout.

Criticality: HIGH

## REQ-PERMISSIONS
Only admin users can open the admin panel; regular users receive 403.

Criticality: CRITICAL
